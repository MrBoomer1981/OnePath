# CLAUDE.md: eval

`eval/` measures how well the radar reads announcements. It is the project's measurement harness, not a test suite: the user decided against tests while the project is in active development and kept eval (in DEEper, where `eval/opps` was made on 25.10.2026). This file covers what it measures, how to run it, and what it relies on. The repository's rules are in the root CLAUDE.md. Paths below are relative to the repository root.

## eval/opps: reading opportunities

- `uv run eval/opps/run_extract.py` measures the extraction (`my_browser/news/opps.py`) on `eval/opps/cases.jsonl`: 69 real posts of the collections of 25–27.10.2026 (Russian and English; hackathons, competitions, internships, schools, fellowships, grants, the Congress of Young Scientists; exact, passed and missing deadlines; restrictions by country; since 27.10.2026 events one comes to: meetups, seminars, a conference for attendees, a slam's audience registration, which were «not opportunities» until then, and Medio Modo's and «Планетарий 1»'s lectures of three cities; and 14 that are not opportunities: results, jobs, memes, webinars for parents, a pupils' training camp, a book of the week, a concert), labeled by hand with the accepted answers: also the topic and region of every opportunity, and the city and an event's start (with its time) where the text decides them. A miss found in use becomes a case (c61 is the congress the radar missed on 26.10.2026).
- It runs them through `opps.describe()` and `opps.read()`, with today fixed at 25.10.2026, and the deadline through `opps.deadline()`: the prompt and the parsing the loop uses. It prints the share right of «is an opportunity» over all cases, and over the opportunities the deadline's day (none when the text gives none), `ru_access`, the type, `closed`, the topic, the region, and over the cases labeled the city (as the loop stores it, `config.city_name()`) and the start; a missed opportunity misses every field. `--only c02,c06` runs those cases alone: a cheap look at misses after a fix. `major` (especially big: the app shows it six months ahead instead of three) has no labels: the results list the cases the model marked, to be read by eye (17 of 44 on 26.10.2026: MATS, Yandex Cup, EEML, Yandex's and Kaspersky's internships, the Potanin scholarship; local hackathons and case championships not). Results go to `eval/opps/results/<date>_<time>_<label>.md|json` (the json has every answer). The last full one, `2026-10-27_2310_events`, read 66 of 69 right (topic 51 of 55, region 52 of 55, city 32 of 36, start 9 of 9): three hackathons for pupils were dropped because of the prompt's «only for pupils», since removed, and three «misses» were the labels' (a final in Magas, a hackathon's offline part in Khanty-Mansiysk, a championship whose tracks are mostly AI), since corrected; `2026-10-27_2312_events-fix`, the changed cases and every event after the fix, read 18 of 18. The one before, `2026-10-26_2313_forums`, read 63 of 63 under the old rules.
- The cases are read against the profile in the settings of its throwaway database: the neutral default, not the user's (the repository is public). `fit` is not measured.
- It costs about 1.5 ₽ a run off-peak (real Flash, 12 calls; 1.46 ₽ on 27.10.2026); ask the user before running it. Its spend goes to a throwaway `REPORTS_DIR`, never to the app's budget. The key: `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` from the environment or this checkout's `.env`; the user's Mac has none here, so pass DEEper's (the same key, from its `.env`) for the run alone.
- After changing `opps.EXTRACT_PROMPT`, `describe()` or `deadline()`, rerun it and read the misses, not just the shares: a label may be the thing to fix. Compare runs made off-peak: DeepSeek doubles its price 01–04 and 06–10 UTC on weekdays, and its speed changes over the day too.

DEEper keeps its own evals of research quality (`eval/run.py`) and of merging AI news (`eval/news`), which chose the embedding model the radar uses.
