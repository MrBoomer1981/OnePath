# CLAUDE.md: eval

`eval/` measures how well the radar reads announcements. It is the project's measurement harness, not a test suite: the user decided against tests while the project is in active development and kept eval (in DEEper, where `eval/opps` was made on 25.10.2026). This file covers what it measures, how to run it, and what it relies on. The repository's rules are in the root CLAUDE.md. Paths below are relative to the repository root.

## eval/opps: reading opportunities

- `uv run eval/opps/run_extract.py` measures the extraction (`my_browser/news/opps.py`) on `eval/opps/cases.jsonl`: 63 real posts of the collections of 25 and 26.10.2026 (Russian and English; hackathons, competitions, internships, schools, fellowships, grants, the Congress of Young Scientists; exact, passed and missing deadlines; restrictions by country; and 18 that are not opportunities: results, meetups, lectures, a slam's audience registration, jobs, memes), labeled by hand with the accepted answers. A miss found in use becomes a case (c61 is the congress the radar missed on 26.10.2026).
- It runs them through `opps.describe()` and `opps.read()`, with today fixed at 25.10.2026, and the deadline through `opps.deadline()`: the prompt and the parsing the loop uses. It prints the share right of «is an opportunity» over all cases, and over the opportunities the deadline's day (none when the text gives none), `ru_access`, the type and `closed`; a missed opportunity misses every field. `major` (especially big: the app shows it six months ahead instead of three) has no labels: the results list the cases the model marked, to be read by eye (17 of 44 on 26.10.2026: MATS, Yandex Cup, EEML, Yandex's and Kaspersky's internships, the Potanin scholarship; local hackathons and case championships not). Results go to `eval/opps/results/<date>_<time>_<label>.md|json` (the json has every answer); the last one, `2026-10-26_2313_forums`, read 63 of 63 right.
- The cases are read against the profile in the settings of its throwaway database: the neutral default, not the user's (the repository is public). `fit` is not measured.
- It costs about 1 ₽ a run (real Flash, 10 calls); ask the user before running it. Its spend goes to a throwaway `REPORTS_DIR`, never to the app's budget.
- After changing `opps.EXTRACT_PROMPT`, `describe()` or `deadline()`, rerun it and read the misses, not just the shares: a label may be the thing to fix. Compare runs made off-peak: DeepSeek doubles its price 01–04 and 06–10 UTC on weekdays, and its speed changes over the day too.

DEEper keeps its own evals of research quality (`eval/run.py`) and of merging AI news (`eval/news`), which chose the embedding model the radar uses.
